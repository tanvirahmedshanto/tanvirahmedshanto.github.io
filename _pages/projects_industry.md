# Super Petrochemical Limited

**Mechanical Engineer** · [Super Petrochemical Limited](https://sppl.com.bd/) · Bangladesh · October 2018 to October 2019

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/industry/spl-plant-night.jpg" alt="Petrochemical distillation columns lit at night" caption="The plant at night." zoomable=true %}

A year covering process equipment design, piping stress analysis, capital project support, and workshop
operations, where designs had to survive fabrication, installation, and maintenance in the field.

## Mooring and pipeline capital programme

Supported development of a **$10M mooring and pipeline programme**, including proposal review across
component drawings, assembly manufacturing instructions, bills of materials, and supporting analysis.

## Process equipment and piping design

Designed process equipment and piping layouts in **SolidWorks** and **AutoCAD Plant 3D**, and performed
piping stress analysis in **CAESAR II**. Worked with fabrication and installation teams so designs were
manufacturable, not just correct on paper.

## Oil tanker construction and maintenance

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/industry/spl-drydock.jpg" alt="Barge and tanker sections under construction in a dry dock" caption="Vessel sections under construction in dry dock." zoomable=true %}

Identified process waste and bottlenecks, improved planning and vendor coordination, and implemented
continuous improvement measures. Construction and maintenance costs fell by roughly **10%**.

## Workshop and maintenance operations

Coordinated a **70-person** mechanical workshop and maintenance crew: prioritising work orders,
allocating resources, and enforcing safe, high-quality repairs. Applied Lean Six Sigma methods including
root cause analysis, DMAIC, SPC, Kaizen, 5-Whys, 5S, and 8D.

# Increscent, Inc.

**Co-Founder and Technical Lead** · [Increscent, Inc.](https://www.increscent3d.com/) · Texas, USA · August 2022 to present

Commercializing the in-situ annealing printing method covered by
[U.S. Patent 12,496,776 B2]({{ '/research/patents/' | relative_url }}).

## Commercialization roadmap

Translated a laboratory printing method into a staged R&D and commercialization roadmap with measurable
performance milestones and qualification criteria, turning a research result into something a customer
can specify and buy.

## Grants and proposals

## Customer discovery

use cases, and qualification criteria. Prepared technical white papers, test reports, pitch decks, and
partner materials.
